# Dictating a text

This guide shows you how to record a dictation in the editor, correct the text, and hand it off
to the application where it belongs.

## Before you start

You are signed in to MIRA Desktop, and the **Editor** shows the text area.

You also need:

- **A speech recognition license.** Without one, the microphone button is disabled and its hint
  reads *No speech recognition license*.
- **A working microphone.** MIRA uses your system's default microphone unless you chose another
  one under **Settings → Recording devices**.

## Step by step

1. **Place the cursor.** Click into the text area.
2. **Start the recording.** Select **Dictate**, or press **Ctrl+Space**. The button turns into
   **Stop recording**, and screen readers announce *Recording started*.
3. **Speak your text.** Each finished phrase appears at the cursor.
4. **Stop the recording.** Select **Stop recording**, or press **Ctrl+Space** again.
5. **Hand the text off.** Select **Cut**. The text area is empty, MIRA confirms with *Cut*, and
   you paste the text into the target application with **Ctrl+V**.

> **Tip:** If the connection drops while you speak, keep talking. MIRA reconnects on its own and
> recognizes the kept audio.

## More options

- **Copy only a part.** Select a passage first; **Copy** and **Cut** then act on that passage
  only.

## If it doesn't work

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| *No microphone found. Please connect a recording device.* | No microphone is available. | Connect a microphone. |
| *You don't have a speech recognition license. Please contact your administrator.* | Your account has no speech recognition license. | Contact your administrator. |

If a problem persists after these steps, contact your administrator.
